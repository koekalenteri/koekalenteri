## New

### Team competitions

- A team is made by choosing its first dog under the team cards, in **{t:teams.newTeam}**. The
  number of teams field is gone.
- Any team can be removed with **Remove team** on its card. The team's dogs are freed, and the
  later teams move up a number. Before, a team could only be removed by lowering the number of
  teams, and that always removed the last one.
- The entry form no longer asks whether the team is a first team. The trial secretary marks the
  first teams on the teams page with *{t:teams.primary}*.

## Fixed

### For the participant

- When a new version of the service comes out during use, the page reloads by itself after every
  update. If part of the page still cannot be loaded, the page says the app has been updated and
  offers **{t:error.reloadPage}**. Before, a tab that had already reloaded once for an update could
  show an error page in English after the next update.

### For the trial secretary

- The trial's form warns when a day of the trial has no class chosen, since no entries can be made
  for such a day. Before, a Sunday added to a trial, for example, could go without classes
  unnoticed, and entries could only be made for Saturday.
- When places are given per class because the trial's classes already have saved places, the form
  says so and tells what to do. Before, the choice simply could not be changed.
- The admin pages of another club's trial say that the event is not found, and no longer keep
  loading or show an error.
- The event's details dialog has one scroll bar, and no empty area at its end any more.
