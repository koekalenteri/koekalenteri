## New

### Team competitions

- A trial can hold a team competition. The trial secretary puts the teams together with
  **{t:eventManagement.teams}**: the number of teams, their names, first teams and dogs. Each dog
  gets a mark in its team, for example 1A.
- The teams page works on a phone too, so the teams can be put together in the trial office during
  the trial day. A dog is found by its name, registration number, handler's name or start number.
- The entry form can ask for the dog's team. From the names given, the teams page suggests teams,
  and in a trial like a derby it also suggests teams of dogs from the same litter.
- A whole team can be moved to the participants or to the reserve list. When there are more teams
  than the trial has room for, the places left can be drawn between the teams with
  **{t:teams.draw}**. First teams are drawn first.
- The public start list shows a team competition by team.
- In a NOWT trial a team's points are added up and the teams are placed. A tie for first place is
  settled by a run-off, whose winner the secretary marks.
- Teams can be split into series that are placed separately, and a team's points can count only
  some of the trial's days, for example the qualifying day without the final.

### Unofficial trials

- An unofficial trial can have *{t:event.ownClasses.title}*, for example YKS and VET, named in
  Finnish and English. Entrants, the start list and the e-mails show the classes by name and in the
  organizer's order.
- An unofficial trial can follow an official trial (NOU, NOME-B, NOME-A or NOWT) with
  *{t:event.officialFormat}*. Its classes, entry, posts and results entry then work as in that
  trial, and it needs no judges.
- In an unofficial trial that follows NOWT, an exercise can have another maximum, and points can be
  given in halves. They are set on the posts page under **{t:event.exerciseScoringTitle}**.

### Editing a trial

- The trial's edit page asks before an unsaved change is lost, like the posts page.
- The edit page, the posts page, the teams page and the event's details dialog each keep their own
  unfinished change. Saving the details dialog no longer carries along a change left unsaved in
  another view, and Esc or a click outside the dialog discards its change like **{t:cancel}**.
- When the trial's days change, the posts with their groups, the days' groups and the places per
  day move with the days, and what was on a day the trial no longer has is removed. Before, they
  stayed on the old days and could not be removed.

### Entering results

- A post's page tells when the running order has changed and reads the list again. Before, every
  save failed until the page was reloaded.
- When a post's save meets another writer's change, the page asks which version stands, as the
  results page does.
- A post's save no longer wipes a stop or a disqualification recorded at another post.
- A whole-round save on the results page keeps the points and stop a post saved during the edit,
  and a correction at one exercise no longer gives the other posts a needless conflict.
- When a dog has two exercises at the same post, saving the second no longer wipes the points of
  the first.
- Two results saved for the same dog at the same time no longer undo each other unnoticed: the
  later one gets a conflict.
- In the results page's view limited to one post, points typed for another post's exercise no
  longer disappear. The page warns, and the points are saved in the view *{t:results.scopeAll}*.
- Choosing a stop no longer changes the dog's judge.
- The reason for a zero no longer shows in red, and a chosen reason can be cleared with
  *{t:results.zeroFaultUnknown}*.
- The results, posts and start number entry pages show which trial is being handled, and the posts'
  entry has a way back to the event.
- The public start list and the secretary's views fetch the changes made during a lost connection
  when it comes back, also when a phone wakes from sleep.

### Sending results to Omakoira

- The registration number of a dog entered by hand can be corrected after the trial. The field was
  locked. The number locks once the dog's result has been sent to the Kennel Club.

### Payments

- The secretary sees payments charged twice above the class lists and can refund them.
- The payment link of an entry paid twice says the payment is waiting for a refund, and no longer
  advises trying again.
- An old unfinished entry's payment link no longer takes a payment when the dog already has a paid
  entry in the same trial.
- If a return from the payment service cannot be verified, the page says so and offers the payment
  again instead of waiting for a confirmation.
- A refund counts refunds still in progress as well, so the same payment cannot be refunded twice.

### Statistics

- Trials by class downloads also for a season with a tentative trial without a price. Season 2025
  did not download before.
- The organizer's statistics are rebuilt every night, and they no longer count unfinished entries.

## Fixed

### For the participant

- Signed in, the public start list and your own entry update by themselves again.
- A tab left open for a long time notices a new version when you return to it.
- A signed-in user without admin rights is told so on the admin pages, and the page no longer keeps
  loading.

### For the trial secretary

- An invitation attachment must be a single PDF of at most 5 MB, and a file too large is reported.
- The entry's change history no longer shows a change of the handler's membership that nobody made.

### For the administrator

- A judge or trial officer whose e-mail address ends in a newer ending, such as .web, is no longer
  left out of the users.
