## New

### Sending results to Omakoira

- The trial secretary sends the trial's results to the Finnish Kennel Club's Omakoira straight from
  Koekalenteri, and no longer has to enter them in Omakoira by hand. Sending is a step of its own
  among the event's steps, **{t:event.states.sendResultsToKennelliitto}**, and the results are
  finally handed over to the chief judge with **{t:results.kennelliitto.handOverButton}**.
- Before sending, the secretary sees a preview: which results go, which are unchanged, and which
  cannot go and why. The dogs are listed in start list order, and the send report also names the
  dogs that could not be sent.
- A foreign dog the Kennel Club does not know yet is added to the Kennel Club before its result is
  sent. The dog is looked up first by its registration number, then by its microchip and its name,
  so a dog already known is not added a second time.
- When a dog is not in the Kennel Club's records, the registration shows that its details were
  typed by hand, and the secretary can correct a hand-typed dog's registration number and other
  details before sending.
- Once the results are handed over to the judge, the results pages lock and the results can no
  longer be changed.
- The reminder about certificate and reserve certificate results is shown only for the formats
  that award them.
- If Omakoira does not accept the points of a working test result, the result is sent with 0 points,
  and Koekalenteri tells which dogs' points must be entered in Omakoira by hand.
- The trial is linked to its Kennel Club trial with **{t:event.kcIdLookup}**. The choice's columns are
  wider than before, and a one-day trial is found correctly even when the Kennel Club gives no end
  date for it.

### Entering and publishing results

- A working test can be given a *{t:event.rotation.title}*: at which posts and in which order each
  group does its exercises. A group can be a class or part of a class's start numbers, an exercise
  can be split in two parts, and a group can have its own number of dogs at once. The next group's
  running order is suggested from the previous one.
- A working test result is worked out by the rules in force (15.4.2023): the prize comes from the
  percentage alone, and a first prize no longer needs half the points of every exercise. An
  eliminating fault gives a nought, and the reasons for a zero follow the rules' list.
- A working test round can record that the handler withdrew, the dog was injured or the dog fell
  ill. A round that ended early keeps the points earned so far.
- *{t:results.zeroFaults.unknown}* can be chosen as the reason for a zero in a working test
  exercise, when the scorecard does not tell it.
- Awarded NOWT dogs get a placement within their class and day. On the public start list, a working
  test result is followed by its points, the maximum, the percentage and the placement.
- When the posts or the running order are changed after results have been recorded, the stored
  results are worked out again, and Koekalenteri asks before it drops points whose exercise no
  longer exists.
- The question about a stopped trial is asked only for formats where a trial can be stopped.
- The result entry table puts each dog and handler on one row, so a whole class fits on the
  screen: the maximum sits beside the score field, and a post's lone judge is named in the
  column heading instead of under every field.
- NOME-A results show the way KoiraNet writes them, for example A1.
- An ended trial stays in the event list until its results are published. Finished trials are
  shown with **{t:showFinishedEvents}**.
- A class's results on a two-day trial are published one day at a time. Before, publishing the
  first day showed the second day's results as soon as they were entered.
- Results cannot be published for a day with no results entered.
- A class's published start list and results show after the trial even when the class's invitations
  were never sent.
- In the calendar, a trial with published results shows the link to its results also when its
  invitations were never sent.
- An open public start list no longer shows an outdated state when two changes are saved at the
  same time.
- A failed save of the results is shown to the secretary also when the save is made from the
  conflict dialog.

### Start numbers

- Start numbers can be published from the trial's first day, even if an invitation is still unsent.
  Before, publishing waited until the day after the trial.
- A published start list and start numbers can be hidden even if an invitation is still unsent.

### Refunds

- The refund dialog itemises the registration's purchases: the entry fee, optional services, what
  was paid, what was refunded and what is still to be paid. Its title names the dog instead of its
  registration number.
- An overpaid registration has a mark of its own in the registration list, so a refund can be told
  from a missing payment without opening the row.
- A dog left on the reserve list whose fee was refunded no longer shows the entry fee as owed.

### Registration form

- Only a 15-digit microchip is accepted for a foreign dog, and a registration number typed by hand
  must be in its registry's form.
- Correcting the handler's contact details no longer removes the handler's membership.
- The breeder and owner sections no longer flash open when the form opens.

### The trial secretary's views in English

- Read in English, the registration's change history, the last message in the participant list,
  the event's name in the secretary's view, the application's update notice and the payment and
  refund error messages are in English.
- The invitation page is shown wholly in one language. The registration's language is switched to
  only when coming from an email link, and the language can be changed from the menu after that.

### Statistics

- Judge workload is shown as a table, with held and upcoming trials apart.
- Participation figures count starts, not registrations: cancelled entries and dogs left on the
  reserve list no longer show as participants.
- The organizer's chart of reserves and cancellations counts only trials that have been held.

### For the participant

- An entry can be paid for up to a week after the trial's last day.
- The participant's own registration page updates at once when the place notice goes out, a payment
  is refunded, the dog is lifted from the reserve list or the secretary changes the registration.

### For the trial secretary

- The trial's price and payment time can no longer be changed once the trial has registrations.

## Fixed

### For the participant

- Returning from the payment service no longer shows an error after a successful payment.
- A registration can be edited and a place confirmed also on the trial's last day.
- A deleted trial no longer shows in the calendar's search nor opens from a link, and it disappears
  from an open search page at once.
- Dates show and save correctly also when the browser is in a time zone other than Finland's. This
  covers the dog's age limit and the qualification period of championship trials too.
- A date typed into the search's *{t:daterangeEnd}* field that is the same as the start date stays.
- Every text of the login window is in Finnish.
- Success notices are readable in contrast, and the page headings are in the right order for a
  screen reader.

### For the trial secretary

- A NOME-B championship trial's qualification period starts from the original end of entry also
  when the previous trial's entry was extended.
- A copy of a trial moves the dates, the places per day and the posts' days correctly also across a
  change to or from summer time, and no longer inherits the original trial's invitation attachments.
- A January trial created or edited in December shows in the event list at once.
- A new trial takes its place in date order in the event list without reloading the page.
- A trial's classes always show in the order ALO, AVO, VOI.
- Clearing a judge from the selection field also removes the judge from the classes and from the
  start list's heading.
- The group view and the server place a registration in the same group.
- The handler's membership shows in the participant list by the same rule the price and the
  priority are worked out by.
- A successful save no longer shows an error when only the update of open pages fails.
- A slow server response, such as the trial ID lookup, no longer gives up after 10 seconds.

## Faster

- The secretary's event list fetches only the changed trials after the first load.
