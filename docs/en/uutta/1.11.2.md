## New

- Publishing the start numbers has a section of its own in the *{t:eventManagement.tabs.management}*
  panel, with **{t:eventManagement.enterStartNumbers}** beside it.
- When the start list cannot be published yet, the panel says why next to the button: pick the
  participants first, or send the invitations first.
- Entering the start numbers starts with the day and only then the class, and the day stays picked
  from one class to the next.
- In results entry the dogs are in their drawn start number order, and a multi-day class is entered
  one day at a time.

## Fixed

- A published day shows on an open public start list without reloading the page.
- In the statistics a trial counts towards the capacity and club figures only once it has been held,
  and the cancellation rate counts the entries left on the reserve list among the registrations.
